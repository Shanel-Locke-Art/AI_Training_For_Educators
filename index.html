How to Play

Observe, predict, test, and revise.

01

Read the teaching problem

Professor Pixel introduces the scenario and points you toward the design problem that matters.

02

Make a deliberate choice

Build a prompt, identify a risk, or predict what the AI is likely to produce before seeing the answer.

03

Evaluate the output

Babbage provides AI-generated analysis, but your professional judgment determines what is useful, incomplete, or unsafe.

04

Revise and continue

Use the feedback to improve the prompt or decision, then carry that reasoning into the next scenario.

About PromptCraft

A visual novel about using AI with intention.

PromptCraft combines scenario-based practice, reflective decision-making, and game-informed faculty development.

The project

PromptCraft

PromptCraft is a faculty-development experience focused on prompt design, course context, critical evaluation, and the limits of generative AI. Learners work through realistic teaching problems with Professor Pixel, consult Babbage, and revise their decisions using evidence and professional judgment.

Developer

Shanel Lara Locke, MFA

Shanel is an instructional designer in the Teaching & Learning Center at Great Falls College MSU. Her work supports faculty development, online course design, digital accessibility, course review, instructional technology, and thoughtful uses of AI in teaching and learning.

Meet Babbage

The machine can analyze. You still have to think.

Babbage is PromptCraft's fictional AI analysis engine: part retro computer, part instructional-design critic, and deliberately not an oracle.

Why Babbage?

A small homage to the long history of computing

The name nods to Charles Babbage, whose nineteenth-century designs for mechanical calculating engines helped shape later ideas about programmable computation. The 1837 detail in the visual identity is a quiet reference to the period in which Babbage described the Analytical Engine.

PromptCraft borrows the name for a fictional system rather than presenting the AI model itself as a character. That keeps the game's identity stable even when the underlying technology changes.

PIXEL

Professor Pixel teaches

Pixel frames the instructional problem, asks you to make a decision, and helps connect the experience back to teaching practice.

BABBAGE

Babbage analyzes

Babbage examines the information you provide, challenges weak assumptions, and generates feedback or a revised design for you to inspect.

YOU

You decide

AI output is evidence to evaluate, not an answer key. You decide whether Babbage's reasoning is useful, incomplete, biased, impractical, or simply wrong.

Behind the machine

Babbage is a fictional interface layered over a generative AI service. During development, the underlying provider or model may change. PromptCraft should identify that technical provider separately rather than pretending the fictional character is the commercial AI system itself.

Human judgment required

Babbage can generate confident but incomplete or incorrect feedback. The learning goal is not to obey the machine. It is to practice giving useful context, noticing what the AI does with it, and deciding what deserves to survive revision.

Research & Privacy

Know what the prototype records.

This plain-language notice describes the current development build. It is not a substitute for formal informed consent.

Information that may be collected

PromptCraft may record an optional name or participant identifier, scenario choices, prompts and revisions, Babbage-generated AI responses, scores, predictions, reflections, timestamps, session duration, completion progress, and basic display information such as screen width.

Why the information is collected

Information may be used to test the prototype, identify usability problems, improve the instructional design, evaluate learning activities, and support future research when the required institutional review and participant-consent processes are in place.

Do not enter confidential information

Do not include student names, grades, course records, health information, employment records, passwords, or other private or legally protected information in any prompt or reflection. The name field may be skipped or replaced with a non-identifying participant code.

AI and external processing

Babbage is the fictional analysis interface used inside PromptCraft. Depending on how PromptCraft is deployed, text submitted to Babbage may be processed by an external generative AI service. The current Babbage development backend uses the OpenAI API. The technical provider may change during development, so treat every prompt as information that could leave the local browser and use fictional or generalized teaching examples rather than identifiable student information.

Formal research participation

This menu notice does not indicate that a formal study has been approved, and it does not serve as informed consent. If PromptCraft is used in an approved research study, participants will receive separate information describing the study purpose, procedures, risks, benefits, confidentiality protections, data use, storage, voluntary participation, and researcher contacts before their data are used for research.
